The Chronicle Story

Deliberate analysis, independent truth.

Founded in Madison to serve the public interest through careful, investigative reporting and local clarity.

The Daily Chronicle emerged from a collective desire to restore patience to local journalism. In an era dominated by instantaneous headlines and algorithmic urgency, we chose a different path: slow, thorough, and completely independent investigative writing. Our founders believed that a community deserves news that has been verified, contextualized, and deeply understood.

We operate free from corporate oversight and partisan pressure. This rare independence means we answer solely to our readers. Our funding model relies primarily on community subscriptions, ensuring that our priority remains investigative integrity rather than click-through incentives.

Inside the Madison newsroom

Our Commitment to the Community

Our reporters live and work in the neighborhoods they cover. From school board decisions to environmental updates along the Yahara lakes, we aim to present accurate records of the events shaping Madison. We hold local institutions accountable while celebrating the intellectual and artistic spirit that defines Wisconsin's capital city.

Our Core Charter

To present the raw facts honestly, admit mistakes swiftly, and keep our editorial pages entirely separated from business operations.

1

Fact Verification

We require double-sourced confirmation for every sensitive claim. Rumors find no refuge on our pages.

2

Radical Correction

When errors occur, we correct them transparently at the top of the digital article within hours of verification.

3

Local Focus

We prioritize stories that directly impact residents of Wisconsin, leaving national commentary to others.
